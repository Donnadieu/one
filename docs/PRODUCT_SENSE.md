# Product sense

**Who:** people who have never opened a terminal. Omar, his friends, his business partner. Not a business.
**North star:** simplicity. If a regular person would ask "what does that mean?", rewrite it.

## Journeys
1. Download, open, and get talked through setup out loud. No terminal, no keys.
2. Ask for something by voice or text; helpers do the work in the background.
3. Connect things from the Connections world: passwords, email and calendar (the Mac's own accounts), browser.
4. Keep life in rooms; share one room with a partner.
5. Approve anything that spends, sends, posts or deletes.

## Never
- Send email by itself (drafts only). Spend money without showing the price and getting a yes.
- Show technical permission prompts, jargon, API keys or config to the person.
- Use Telegram or WhatsApp as One's phone path (phone = its own app, later).
- Collect data about people. Everything stays on their Mac.
- Website sign-ins (OAuth reviews) for email/calendar: use the accounts already on the Mac.
- Let anything that isn't ready be used while One is getting ready (after an update, a quit, a crash, a wake, an engine restart): show "Getting ready…", hold what's sent until it opens, never a time estimate, never a forever wait (StartupGate.swift).
