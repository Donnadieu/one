# First-time-user test kit

Goal: watch one person who has never used a terminal install and use One **with no help**.
Every place they stop, frown or ask a question is a bug.

## Before (you, 5 minutes)
1. Pick someone non-technical with a Mac (Apple Silicon, macOS 14+) and a ChatGPT or Claude subscription.
2. Send them only this: "Download One from https://github.com/Donnadieu/one/releases/latest and set it up. Think out loud. I won't help."
3. Sit next to them, or join a screen share. Start a timer. Don't touch anything.

## During: write down every moment like this
| Time | What they were trying | What they did / said | Stuck? (y/n) |
|---|---|---|---|

Only speak if they're stuck for more than 2 minutes. Then write down exactly what you said: that's a fix.

## Tasks to give them after setup (one at a time)
1. "Ask it to tidy your Downloads folder."
2. "Ask it what's on your calendar tomorrow."
3. "Ask it to draft a reply to your latest email." (Check: it is a draft, they press Send.)
4. "Ask it to watch the price of something you want to buy."
5. "Close the window and ask it something by voice."

## After (2 minutes, ask them)
1. What was the most confusing moment?
2. What did you think it would do that it didn't?
3. Would you use it tomorrow? For what?

## Score
- Setup time to first answer: ___ min (goal: under 10)
- Times stuck: ___ (goal: 0)
- Times you had to help: ___ (goal: 0)

Send the notes to Elias ("here are my test notes") and it files each stuck moment into the friction log.
